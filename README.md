# SOC Analyst Career Path, Red Team Operations & Full Kill Chain Simulation CTF Labs

Welcome to the **SOC Analyst Career Path, Red Team Operations & Full Kill Chain Simulation CTF Lab Collection**. This repository serves as an organized, searchable catalog of practical cybersecurity labs hosted on Learn SecByte.

This collection bridges structured SOC analyst career development with live-fire operational simulations—including **SOC Analyst Tier Progression (Level 1, Level 2, Level 3)**, **Blue Team & Cyber Defense Essentials**, **Advanced Red Team Tradecraft (Glass Meridian, Mission Control)**, **Enterprise Breach Simulations (Phishing to Persistence)**, and **Full Kill Chain Exercises (Operation Night Harbor)**.

## Learning Path

### Path A: SOC Analyst Career Path (Tier 1 to Tier 3)
1. **Foundations:** Start with [Intro to Blue Teaming](https://learn.secbyte.org/ctf/intro-to-blue-teaming), [Blue Team Fundamentals](https://learn.secbyte.org/ctf/blue-team-fundamentals), and [Security Operations Center Basics](https://learn.secbyte.org/ctf/security-operations-center-basics).
2. **Tier 1 (Triage):** Master log analysis in [SOC Analyst Level 1: Log Analysis](https://learn.secbyte.org/ctf/soc-analyst-level-1-log-analysis).
3. **Tier 2 (Investigation):** Advance to multi-source triage in [SOC Analyst Level 2: Threat Detection](https://learn.secbyte.org/ctf/soc-analyst-level-2-threat-detection).
4. **Tier 3 (Lead/Hunting):** Master advanced detection in [Cyber Defense Essentials](https://learn.secbyte.org/ctf/cyber-defense-essentials) and [SOC Analyst Level 3: Advanced Threat Detection](https://learn.secbyte.org/ctf/soc-analyst-level-3-advanced-threat-detection).

### Path B: Red Team Operator & Breach Simulation Specialist
1. **Red Team Tradecraft:** Begin with [Red Team Operation Phases: Tradecraft and Mission Control](https://learn.secbyte.org/ctf/red-team-operation-phases-tradecraft-and-mission-control).
2. **Attack Scenarios:** Practice intrusion chains in [Enterprise Breach Simulation: From Phish to Persistence](https://learn.secbyte.org/ctf/enterprise-breach-simulation-from-phish-to-persistence).
3. **Capstone Simulations:** Complete full-scale adversary operations in [Final Red Team Assessment: Operation Glass Meridian](https://learn.secbyte.org/ctf/final-red-team-assessment-operation-glass-meridian) and [Full Kill Chain Simulation: Operation Night Harbor](https://learn.secbyte.org/ctf/full-kill-chain-simulation-operation-night-harbor).

### Path C: Collaborative Defense & Purple Teaming
1. **Live-Fire Exercises:** Conduct joint offense/defense validation in [Purple Team Exercise: Detect, Deceive, Defend](https://learn.secbyte.org/ctf/purple-team-exercise-detect-deceive-defend).

## How to Use the Labs

1. Select a practice scenario from the **Lab Collection Index**.
2. Click the direct link to launch the exercise environment on Learn SecByte.
3. Review scenario directives, alert queues, or target network maps.
4. Triage incoming SIEM alerts, execute red team tradecraft, or conduct collaborative purple team exercises to capture flags.

## Target Audience

* **SOC Analysts (Tiers 1-3):** Progress systematically from entry-level log triage to advanced threat detection and threat hunting.
* **Red Team Operators & Ethical Hackers:** Master C2 infrastructure setups, multi-stage breach chains, and full kill chain simulations.
* **Purple Team Operators & Security Architects:** Run collaborative detection validation exercises using deception technology and live adversary emulation.
* **Cybersecurity Students:** Experience realistic enterprise SOC workflows and advanced threat scenarios.
